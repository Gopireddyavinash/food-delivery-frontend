Our Journey

From a single kitchen concept to an artisanal food movement.

Back in 2023, our founders were frustrated with lukewarm takeaways, wilted salads, and excessive packaging waste. We envisioned a food tech platform engineered around food freshness instead of mere logistics.

Today, CraveNest equips chef-partners with proprietary smart dispatch algorithms that synchronize kitchen prep with rider arrival down to the second. Your hand-tossed pizza leaves the 900-degree stone oven right when the insulated thermal carrier is ready.

The CraveNest Quality Code

The four non-negotiable promises behind every single bite

Farm-to-Fork Purity

We partner exclusively with kitchens that source fresh, ethically harvested local produce and clean cold-pressed oils.

Thermal Freshness Lock

Custom aerogel-insulated thermal delivery bags ensure hot curries remain bubbling and ice creams never melt en route.

100% Eco Packaging

Over 94% of our packaging is biodegradable bagasse, recycled cardboard, and compostable cornstarch cutlery.

Fair Chef Partnerships

Unlike predatory aggregator apps, we empower independent culinary masters with transparent, chef-first commission tiers.
